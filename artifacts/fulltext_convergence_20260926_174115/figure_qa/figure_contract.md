# Appendix figure contract

- Backend: Python/matplotlib, inherited from the existing scripts.
- Reuse: structural and editorial adaptation of the existing design, result,
  and discovery figures. Frozen analysis and archived pair coordinates remain
  unchanged; no experiment, resampling, scoring, or statistical fit is run.
- Output: editable PDF/SVG and 600 dpi PNG in both figure directories. Figure
  widths are 6.8 inches for design/results and 5.8 inches for discovery; use
  sufficient manuscript width to retain at least a 5 pt final text floor.
- Design: a schematic-led composite separating data exposure, the four-arm
  comparison, and implemented admission scope. II-E is development-only.
- Results: quantitative grid; panel a retains all 18 paired cell contrasts
  and the frozen pooled interval, panel b retains the four descriptive core
  means. The right panel has no invented per-arm uncertainty.
- Discovery: one-panel scatter; all 91 archived source-similarity and
  correctness-disagreement pairs are retained. The archived coordinates have
  four-decimal precision. The original published summary annotations are
  retained without recalculation. No program-equivalence claim is made.
- QA: strict rendered panel alignment, source audit, PDF glyph-size audit,
  collision audit, and visual inspection of all panels.
