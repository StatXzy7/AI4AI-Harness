## Alternative text

Three-part illustrated figure. Eight generated programs plus a baseline are compared with nine identical baseline slots. Estimated oracle headroom is 2.33 pp for the generated panel and 2.16 pp for identical-code controls. One hundred task glyphs show persistent scored losses against baseline; one shows a persistent win sensitive to answer extraction. Two repeats freeze the per-task choice and best fixed member, then a held-out repeat tests their gain. Clone-adjusted held-out gain is −0.26 pp, with a 95% interval from −1.45 to +0.95 pp. Specialization remains unresolved. All results use the 386-task complete cohort.

## Interpretation notes

The headroom bars are descriptive repeat-mean plug-in estimates; they do not measure specialization directly. The task-wise mapping and fixed comparator are frozen using discovery outcomes on the same evaluation tasks. D is the difference between the generated and clone arms' held-out selection gains, not the difference between the headroom bars. Its interval is a paired task-bootstrap interval. Three repeats are insufficient to resolve stable complementarity under the reported calibration.
