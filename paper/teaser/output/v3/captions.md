**Coverage, repeatability, and useful selection are distinct.** (a) Eight generated programs plus a single-call baseline are compared with nine byte-identical baseline slots on 386 common MATH-500 tasks, with three fresh repeats per slot. (b) In each of three folds, two repeats determine a frozen per-task member mapping and the best fixed member; the held-out repeat measures their accuracy difference, G. The primary diagnostic is D = Ggen − Gclone. (c) Execution-matched, post-execution oracle coverage reaches 98.70% in both arms at 27 harness executions per task; clone replay averages uniformly over execution subsets. Separately, repeat-mean oracle headroom is positive for both generated and identical-code arms. (d) Residual score-pattern correlation is higher for generated programs, but persistent scored losses against baseline occur on 100 tasks and wins on one extraction-sensitive task. A task is counted when at least one generated member exhibits the difference in all three repeats; each square denotes one task. (e) The clone-adjusted held-out gain is −0.26 pp, with 95% CI [−1.45, +0.95], leaving stable complementarity unresolved. Separately, the tested frozen question-feature selector chooses the baseline for all 386 tasks and gains 0.00 pp over it. Error bars are 95% bootstrap intervals. Execution matching does not match model calls or tokens. Program glyphs and artwork are schematic.

This caption works for both variants. Abbreviations: pp, percentage points; CI, confidence interval; r, residual correlation after removing additive task and member effects. The observed coverage tie is not an equivalence finding. No caption or figure has been inserted into LaTeX.
