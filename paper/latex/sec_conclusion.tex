For the tested MATH panel, more programs do not improve on more rolls
at the full execution budget: both arms reach
$\RcvReplayFullCoverage\%$ oracle coverage after 27 executions, and
the frozen feature selector gains $\RcvPolicyGain$ pp.
Generated programs nevertheless exhibit strongly repeatable score
patterns, with persistent losses spanning far more tasks than
persistent wins. The same-code control, repeated executions, and
frozen selector separate three distinct quantities: available correct
answers, repeatable differences, and usable task advantages.
Useful specialization requires the last of these: advantages that
guide selection and improve on additional executions of a fixed program.
