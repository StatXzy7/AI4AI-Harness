Additional coverage, stable task specialization, and achievable selection
gains are distinct objects; evidence for one cannot substitute for the
others. Here, generated populations supply additional correct answers,
but identical programs also yield positive oracle estimates. Missingness
and low sensitivity leave stable specialization unresolved. The tested
selector gains nothing, and execution-matched replay shows no consistent
portfolio advantage. Population evaluation should connect coverage,
repeat validation, and actual selection while reporting what each establishes.
