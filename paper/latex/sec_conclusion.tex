AI-generated harness populations can be exactly what their source files suggest
and nothing like what routing needs --- and the reverse can also hold. Phase I
measured the syntactic--behavioral gap, traced its mechanisms, and showed the
missing headroom exists on the task. A
pre-specified confirmatory factorial study (six builders, three seeds,
$2{\times}2$ forcing $\times$ gate, 1{,}169 unseen questions) then revised the
story twice. \textbf{Collapse proved protocol-sensitive}: a failure mode of
specific pipelines, invisible to every code-level check they passed.
\textbf{The intervention was null within measurement precision}, its sub-pp
effects sitting below the noise floor. The practical output stands: measure
disagreement, repair, harm, and headroom before routing, select populations
for complementarity rather than accuracy, and audit the generation protocol
itself.
