AI-generated harness populations can be exactly what their source files suggest
and nothing like what routing needs --- and the reverse can also hold. We measured
the syntactic--behavioral gap in a Phase-I discovery population, traced its
mechanisms at the trace level, and showed the missing headroom exists on the task.
A pre-specified confirmatory factorial study --- six builders, three seeds,
$2{\times}2$ strategy forcing $\times$ conformance gate, 1{,}169 questions from
databases never opened during development, official BIRD scorer --- then revised
the story twice.

First, \textbf{collapse proved protocol-sensitive}. The Phase-I collapse regime
did not reproduce under the clean equal-budget protocol: free ungated generation
already produced headroom-rich populations (8.6\,pp mean oracle headroom, 31\%
union repair, 10\% pairwise disagreement). Collapse is a failure mode of
\emph{specific} generation pipelines, not a universal property of AI-generated
harness populations --- which is precisely why code-level checks cannot substitute
for outcome-level auditing.

Second, \textbf{the intervention showed no clear improvement} ($-0.16$\,pp, CI
$[-0.90, +0.82]$; permutation $p = 0.65$). The apparent negative gate main
effect ($-0.67$\,pp unmatched) was substantially size-confounded: the gate
admits $\sim$30\% fewer candidates, and at matched population size the
residual ($-0.30$\,pp, CI $[-0.73, +0.11]$) is not distinguishable from zero,
nor Holm-significant before matching ($p_{\mathrm{Holm}} = 0.084$). The
bare-outcome noise floor under API nondeterminism (14.5\% any-flip rate across
three temperature-0 repeats) exceeds these sub-part-per-point effects. We
therefore do not interpret the residual: on this benchmark, neither strategy
forcing nor conformance gating moved population headroom beyond noise in either
direction.

\textbf{Limitations.} One domain family (text-to-SQL); Phase-I results come
from a discovery design with disclosed task overlap and a proxy judge; inference
is conditional on the six sampled builders; the cache-off arm replication (R2)
is pending. The practical output stands: measure disagreement, repair, harm, and
headroom before routing over a generated population, and audit the generation
protocol --- the collapse we found was invisible to every code-level check the
pipeline had passed.
