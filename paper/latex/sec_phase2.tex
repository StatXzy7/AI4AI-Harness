\paragraph{Same-code slots isolate the sampling explanation.}
Running more members introduces more attempts as well as more code.
We therefore compare nine distinct members with nine byte-identical
\bare{} slots, each evaluated three times per task. The latter preserve
the opportunity for stochastic outcomes while removing program
specialization. They are an equal-ability reference, not an assumption
that all generated programs must behave like \bare{}.

\paragraph{Held-out repeats separate discovery from validation.}
\label{sec:calibration-test}
Choosing a task's best program and validating it on the same outcomes
would reuse lucky successes. Instead, each of three folds uses two
discovery repeats to freeze both a per-task member mapping and a
single best fixed member. The third repeat measures their accuracy
difference. Averaging folds gives $G_{\rm real}$ and $G_{\rm clone}$;
the fixed comparator is discovery-selected, not necessarily \bare{}.
We then compare the arms on their common complete tasks:
\begin{equation}
D=G_{\rm real}-G_{\rm clone}.
\label{eq:clone-difference}
\end{equation}
Here the subtraction is of paired task-level gains. It asks whether
past outcomes identify choices that transfer to another execution
better for distinct programs than for identical ones. Because those
past outcomes come from the same evaluation tasks, this is a repeat
diagnostic, not an unseen-task router.

\paragraph{Missingness and sensitivity govern the interpretation.}
A complete task has a verdict for every member and repeat; unknown
outcomes stay missing. Descriptive quantities use each arm's complete
tasks, while $D$ uses their intersection. The decision rule requires
at most $10\%$ incomplete tasks in either arm and on the intersection,
at least $30$ paired tasks, and positive-effect checks including
$D>1$\,\%. Known-truth simulations assess whether the procedure detects
crossovers at the available repeat count. These checks distinguish an
unresolved result from evidence of absence. Appendix~\ref{app:wp1r} documents completeness;
Appendix~\ref{app:statistics} gives the full rule, tie handling,
uncertainty, and sensitivity analyses.
The acquisition protocol was fixed before collection, with operational
amendments during collection; the v3 analysis was revised afterward.
