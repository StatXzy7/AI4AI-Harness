\subsection{Exploratory MATH population, splits, and coverage}\label{app:crossdomain}

This single-run study establishes the initial coverage phenomenon and
supplies the programs for the repeat panel. It carries the BIRD admission
study's free, ungated generation procedure into competition mathematics,
with a domain-specific answer comparator.

\paragraph{Protocol.} Six builders (the same models as the BIRD admission study), one seed,
eight slots each, an $R{=}3$ raw-attempt cap per slot, neutral validity gate
(mechanism-blind: parse, import, interface, non-empty answer on two dev
questions). Frozen target GLM-5.3-Flash (same as the SQL study), with decoding
settings defined by each frozen member; the baseline uses temperature $0$. MATH-500 \citep{math500}: 500 tasks frozen before generation (100 dev /
400 eval, fixed random split). The 100 dev tasks are used only for
generation-side smoke tests and are never executed in the evaluation
archive; the selection-policy experiments of
Appendix~\ref{app:archive-analysis} therefore carve their training split (80
tasks) from the eval side, leaving 320 scored tasks for D/E claims.
This historical archive analysis is distinct from the fresh repeat
study, whose selector uses the 100 development tasks and scores all
400 evaluation tasks (Appendix~\ref{app:selection}). Judge: numeric equality where both sides parse as
numbers (including $a/b$, \verb|\frac{a}{b}|, decimals), else normalized
LaTeX string equality (units, base subscripts, $^\circ$ stripped;
\verb|\text{X}| content preserved; pmatrix $\to$ tuple). Judge
self-consistency: 400/400 gold answers judge correct against themselves.
single-call accuracy 0.9375 under the matched answer-format instruction. Yield:
35/48 slots admitted ($R_{\mathrm{artifact}}$ 0.10--1.00; the two
reasoning-heavy builders, GLM-5.3 and Qwen3.8-Max, exhausted the 32k-token
generation budget on reasoning before emitting code in most slots ---
reported as-is). This constraint under-represents reasoning-heavy builder styles
in the evaluated pool; we do not adjust the
budget post hoc, and the limitation is part of the reported protocol. This is a replication probe, not a factorial: no
strategy forcing, no conformance gate.

\paragraph{Results (35 harnesses, 400 eval tasks).} Mean pairwise outcome
disagreement 10.6\% (max 33.0\%); 33 of the 36 members had unique outcome
vectors when single-call is counted (32 of the 35 generated harnesses);
outcome-identical pairs 0.7\% --- no trace of
Phase-I-style collapse. Union repair 76.0\% of single-call errors; the union of all
generated candidates fails on 0.8\% of single-call-correct tasks (a
\emph{union-all-fail} rate, not an any-candidate or router harm). Observed
oracle coverage exceeds single-call by 4.75\,\%, and the best fixed member is
\emph{single-call itself} (0.9375; generated harnesses 0.6825--0.915): every
generated member is worse individually. The one-run mini-audit originally
did not collect repeated-execution or same-code clone controls. The
separate WP-1R collection (\S\ref{sec:f3-clones}) adds those controls
on the same task set for eight generated members plus \bare{}, with
nine same-code slots and three fresh repeats. Its acquisition protocol
was frozen before collection, while the v3 analysis was revised later.
Its nonpositive clone-adjusted mean and limited sensitivity do not
retroactively identify the source of the full population's $4.75$\,\%
one-run headroom. A
hash-bound re-execution of the versioned endpoint-sensitive rescore of all
14{,}400 archived verdicts changes 14 of them (0.097\%): all are v1 false
rejects of interval-endpoint answers on a single task, so per-member
accuracies rise by at most one task and no qualitative conclusion moves
(Appendix~\ref{app:archive-analysis}). An earlier stored artifact reporting zero flips is
not reproduced by the corrected analysis and is retained only as provenance.

\paragraph{Scope.} One seed, one target, no factorial, exploratory; the
Phase-I collapse-inducing SQL protocol was not re-run in the math domain,
so this replication speaks to the clean-protocol result only, not to the
boundary of collapse.
