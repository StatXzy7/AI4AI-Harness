\section{Same-Code Controls and Repeated-Execution Protocol}\label{app:wp1r}
This section records acquisition, complete-task estimands, and missing
outcomes. Statistical decisions and calibration are in
Appendix~\ref{app:statistics}; the frozen selector and replay are in
Appendix~\ref{app:selection}.
\paragraph{Frozen protocol.}
The acquisition design was fixed before collection. Statistical and
operational specifications were amended before confirmatory outcomes;
the attempt ceiling increased from $45{,}000$ to $60{,}000$ and concurrency
from $2$ to $6$ during collection. Eligibility requires historical mean
logical calls ${\leq}3.05$, favoring low-call mechanisms rather than a
uniform sample of the $35$ generated members. A fixed salted-hash draw
selects one member per builder and two at-large seats. Both the nine-member
panel and nine byte-identical \bare{} slots receive $R=3$ fresh repeats.
The fixed MATH-500 split contains $400$ evaluation and $100$ development
tasks. Evaluation tasks were used in the earlier one-run study; the new
collection validates fresh executions, not unseen tasks.

\paragraph{Descriptive estimates and paired inference.}
The complete-task oracle estimates are $\WpVthreePlugin$\,\% for the
$350$ complete generated-arm tasks and $\WpVthreeClonePlugin$\,\% for
$383$ complete clone tasks. The earlier values of $2.54$\,\% and
$2.67$\,\% use available-repeat means over all $400$ tasks, filling
fully unobserved means with zero. These are different descriptive
estimands. The main text uses complete tasks; the paired $D$ uses their
$342$-task intersection and remains \textsc{Insufficient Evidence}.

\paragraph{Collection interruptions and missing outcomes.}
Collection used a $60{,}000$-attempt global budget with per-cell atomic
reservation. Two provider-timeout interruptions split the generated arm
across three continuation groups and the clone arm across two. The
solver condition was held fixed across groups. A completed observation
takes precedence over an unresolved request; among completed records
for the same cell, the first is retained.
Across \WpRowsRead{} generated-arm records, \WpMigrationCopies{}
were migration copies. Completed observations replaced unresolved
markers \WpCompletedBeatsUnknown{} times; \WpUnknownThenCompleted{}
earlier unresolved cells were later completed. There were
\WpDoubleExec{} cells with disagreeing completed executions; the primary
analysis retains the first and a sensitivity analysis excludes them.
The clone arm's \WpCloneRowsRead{} records reduce to \WpCloneUnique{}
unique cells, with \WpCloneMigrationCopies{} copies and
\WpCloneConflicts{} conflicts.

\WpUnknownCarried{} cells remain unresolved and missing from the
outcome matrices. The primary $G$ uses complete tasks without imputation.
Sensitivity scenarios fill unknown outcomes with all zeros or all ones
and recompute the full cross-fit, including selection changes.
Three collector versions served the generated-arm continuations with
concurrency $2/2/6$. Program identity and solver conditions were checked
at combination; these operational differences are retained as study
conditions.
