\section{WP-1R/WP-2R Fresh Collection: Protocol and Execution Record}
\label{app:wp1r}

\paragraph{Frozen protocol.} The acquisition protocol
(\texttt{review-stage/REAL\_EVIDENCE\_PROTOCOL\_V1.md}, v1.0 frozen
2026-09-15 plus the v1.1 budget amendment, both committed before any
confirmatory outcome) fixes: the estimands E1--E4 of
\S\ref{sec:wp1r}; the panel draw (eligibility by frozen repeat-0 mean
logical calls ${\leq}3.05$ --- a budget-feasibility filter disclosed as
biasing the panel toward low-call mechanisms, not a uniform sample of the
35 generated members; then a salted-hash draw, one stratum seat per builder
plus two at-large seats, salt \texttt{wp1r-panel-v1-20260915}, recorded in
\texttt{WP1R\_PANEL\_DRAW.json}); $R{=}3$ fresh repeats per arm; the
nine-slot same-code clone arm (\texttt{clone-c1..c9}, byte-identical
\bare{} source) as the exchangeable null; the A8.6 discovery/validation
procedure (discovery repeat 1, validation repeats 2--3, $\delta{=}1.0$\,pp,
10{,}000 permutations, 10{,}000 task bootstrap draws, seed 20260915); the
paired difference and its CI use only the task intersection complete in
both arms; a coverage gate degrades the main verdict to INSUFFICIENT when
either arm excludes more than 10\% of tasks, with two frozen sensitivity
branches (within-task repeat-mean imputation, and exclusion of
doubly-executed conflict cells); and
the acceptance rule that any of SUPPORTED / REFUTED / INSUFFICIENT
terminates the study without re-running or re-sampling. The 400 eval and
100 dev tasks are the frozen MATH-500 split (SHA256 recorded); eval tasks
were analyzed before in the one-run mini-audit, so results are labeled
retrospective-task/prospective-execution, not unseen-task validation.

\paragraph{Execution record and continuation merge.} Collection ran
2026-09-15 through 2026-09-19 under a 60{,}000-attempt global budget with
per-cell atomic reservation. Two provider-timeout stops (2026-09-17,
2026-09-18) split the real arm across three continuation ledgers and the
clone arm across two; each continuation re-binds the identical frozen
solver condition (verified field-by-field at merge). The merge rule is
deterministic: a completed observation beats an \texttt{unknown\_remote}
marker; among completed records of the same cell the earliest ledger wins.
Across the real arm's
\WpRowsRead{} rows it collapsed \WpMigrationCopies{} migration
copies, preferred a completed record over an unknown-remote marker
\WpCompletedBeatsUnknown{} times, completed an earlier unknown marker
\WpUnknownThenCompleted{} times, and found \WpDoubleExec{} cells with two
completed but disagreeing executions (first-completed retained; a frozen
sensitivity branch excludes them instead). The clone arm's
\WpCloneRowsRead{} rows collapsed to 10{,}800 unique cells
(\WpCloneMigrationCopies{} copies, zero conflicts).
\WpUnknownCarried{} cells remain
\texttt{unknown\_remote} across all ledgers and stay NaN in the outcome
matrices --- never imputed --- so classes requiring those cells abstain by
the frozen rules. The merged arms are hash-sealed
(\texttt{SEALED\_eval\_real.json}, \texttt{SEALED\_eval\_clone.json},
\texttt{SEALED\_dev\_real.json}); the analysis code and its unit tests are
in \texttt{experiment/revision/wp1r\_analysis.py}.

\paragraph{Accounting (E4).} Per-request accounting layers
experiment-run $>$ harness-execution $>$ logical-call $>$ provider-attempt;
migration copies are excluded from the totals (distinct re-executions count
individually). Totals: \WpLogicalCalls{} logical calls, \WpHttpAttempts{}
provider attempts, \WpTotalTokens{} known tokens, conservative cost ceiling
\WpCostCny{} CNY at the frozen break-even price of 44.3 CNY per 1M tokens
(pricing not independently verifiable; reported as token counts times the
frozen price, never as billed amounts).

\paragraph{Selector spec (E3).} $\pi_Z$ is multinomial logistic regression
over TF-IDF question features (max 300) and per-member dev accuracies
computed within training folds only; 5-fold task-level CV on the 100 dev
tasks with same-task repeats never split across folds; fold-model
averaging; ties broken by member id; abstain to \bare{} when the top-2
probability gap is below 0.15. Comparators: \bare{}, dev-fixed, pre-fixed
random member (seeded), M1b (dev-fixed with bare fallback on missing
cells). $E_1$ (budget by construction) is not applicable because panel
members make more than one call; $E_b$ with $b{=}3$ calls per task is
reported as a separately named estimand. Leakage controls: no answer,
correctness, eval-derived difficulty, post-execution trace, or judge
feature is used.
