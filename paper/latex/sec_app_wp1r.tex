\section{WP-1R/WP-2R Fresh Collection: Protocol and Execution Record}
\label{app:wp1r}

\paragraph{Frozen protocol.} The acquisition protocol
(\texttt{review-stage/REAL\_EVIDENCE\_PROTOCOL\_V1.md}, v1.0 frozen
2026-09-15, with dated amendments: v1.1 (statistical and budget fixes)
and v1.2 (operational test specification) preceded any confirmatory
outcome; v1.3 (attempt ceiling 45{,}000$\to$60{,}000, matching the task
authorization) and the concurrency amendment (2$\to$6) were made
mid-collection for throughput and are disclosed as condition-relevant
history) fixes: the panel draw (eligibility by frozen repeat-0 mean
logical calls ${\leq}3.05$ --- a budget-feasibility filter disclosed as
biasing the panel toward low-call mechanisms, not a uniform sample of the
35 generated members; then a salted-hash draw, one stratum seat per builder
plus two at-large seats, salt \texttt{wp1r-panel-v1-20260915}, recorded in
\texttt{WP1R\_PANEL\_DRAW.json}); $R{=}3$ fresh repeats per arm; the
nine-slot same-code clone arm (\texttt{clone-c1..c9}, byte-identical
\bare{} source) as the exchangeable equal-ability reference; the 400 eval
and 100 dev tasks of the frozen MATH-500 split (SHA256 recorded); eval
tasks were analyzed before in the one-run mini-audit, so results are
labeled retrospective-task/prospective-execution, not unseen-task
validation.

\paragraph{Statistical procedure (v3, post 2026-09-19 review).}
The primary complementarity statistic is the cross-fitted
frozen-selection gain $G$ of \S\ref{sec:calibration-test}: in each of
three folds, two repeats freeze the per-task member mapping and the best
\emph{fixed} member, the third repeat scores the frozen mapping against
that fixed member; $G$ is the per-task mean averaged over folds.
Tie-breaking is label-symmetric: because $R{=}2$ discovery scores lie
on $\{0,\tfrac12,1\}$ and ties dominate at high accuracy, a deterministic
lexicographic rule would bias the selection toward the first listed
member, so iid $U(0,10^{-9})$ jitter is drawn per (fold, task) and
averaged over ten frozen tie realizations; a simulation over
dominant-member positions verifies the statistic is order-independent.
The decision statistic is the real-minus-clone difference
$D=G_{\rm real}-G_{\rm clone}$ on the task intersection complete in both
arms. Its reference distribution uses $10{,}000$ two-arm randomizations:
within every task and repeat, member labels are permuted independently
in each arm and $D$ is recomputed (a single permutation joint across
repeats was rejected in development because it preserves a genuine
crossover mapping and does not null it). A paired task bootstrap
(2{,}000 draws, seed 20260915) gives the 95\% interval. The frozen rule
emits SUPPORTED only when the coverage gate passes ($\le10\%$ incomplete
tasks in either arm; $\ge30$ paired tasks), the randomization $p<0.05$,
the bootstrap lower bound is positive, $D>1.0$\,pp, and $G_{\rm real}$'s
own lower bound is positive; REFUTED requires the $D$ upper bound within
the margin and a small equivalence $p$; otherwise INSUFFICIENT. The
acceptance rule that any of these states terminates the study without
re-running remains. The earlier v2 procedure --- a discovery-repeat
argmax scored against \bare{} on repeats 2--3 (A8.6) --- is retained as
a labeled secondary statistic, not a complementarity test (under pure
global dominance with \bare{} at 0.5 and the other members at 0.9 its
expectation is $\approx0.5(1-0.1^8)\cdot0.4\approx0.20$). Two frozen
sensitivity branches accompany it (within-task available-repeat mean
imputation; exclusion of doubly-executed conflict cells).

\paragraph{Execution record and continuation merge.} Collection ran
2026-09-15 through 2026-09-19 under a 60{,}000-attempt global budget with
per-cell atomic reservation. Two provider-timeout stops (2026-09-17,
2026-09-18) split the real arm across three continuation ledgers and the
clone arm across two; each continuation re-binds the identical frozen
solver condition (verified field-by-field at merge). The merge rule is
deterministic: a completed observation beats an \texttt{unknown\_remote}
marker; among completed records of the same cell the earliest ledger wins.
Across the real arm's
\WpRowsRead{} rows it collapsed \WpMigrationCopies{} migration
copies, preferred a completed record over an unknown-remote marker
\WpCompletedBeatsUnknown{} times, completed an earlier unknown marker
\WpUnknownThenCompleted{} times, and found \WpDoubleExec{} cells with two
completed but disagreeing executions (first-completed retained; a frozen
sensitivity branch excludes them instead). The clone arm's
\WpCloneRowsRead{} rows collapsed to \WpCloneUnique{} unique cells
(\WpCloneMigrationCopies{} copies, \WpCloneConflicts{} conflicts).
\WpUnknownCarried{} cells remain
\texttt{unknown\_remote} across all ledgers and stay NaN in the outcome
matrices --- never imputed --- so the v3 coverage gate excludes their
tasks (point $G$ is a complete-case statistic, exact on the included
tasks), and a scenario range for $G$ under all-0 and all-1 filling of
the excluded tasks' unknown outcomes (the tight fill-extremes, each
recomputing the full cross-fit including selection changes) is reported
as \texttt{missing\_extremal\_G\_bounds}. The merged arms
are hash-sealed (\texttt{SEALED\_eval\_real.json},
\texttt{SEALED\_eval\_clone.json}, \texttt{SEALED\_dev\_real.json}).
Three collector source versions served the real arm across continuations
(concurrency 2/2/6); each seal records the arm's bound collector hash,
and the identity gates exclude run-identity fields (acquisition id)
while this appendix discloses the concurrency and amendment history.
The analysis code, calibration simulation, and their unit tests are in
\texttt{experiment/diagnostics/ccomp\_v3.py},
\texttt{experiment/diagnostics/calibration\_sim.py}, and
\texttt{experiment/revision/wp1r\_analysis.py}.

\paragraph{Budget semantics and realized accounting (E4).}
Three distinct comparisons are reported and never conflated.
(i) \emph{Equal-slot collection}: both arms execute nine members/slots
for three repeats; this is the randomization design and does not imply
matched spend. (ii) \emph{Common per-task execution budget}
(\S\ref{sec:f3-clones}): offline comparison at
$b\in\{3,9,15,27\}$ executions per task between the dev-ranked panel
portfolio and $b$ of the 27 same-code executions, under post-execution
\emph{oracle} aggregation only; panel-minus-same-code oracle differences
are $+0.37,+0.03,-0.11,-0.25$\,pp. No cross-arm majority-vote accuracy is
reported because the arms emit incompatible answer formats (boxed numbers
versus free-form prose), so a modal answer key would be unfair. (iii) \emph{Realized spend}: per-request
accounting layers experiment-run $>$ harness-execution $>$ logical-call
$>$ provider-attempt; migration copies are excluded. The real arm made
22{,}192 logical calls (32{,}097 provider attempts; 54.17\,M known
tokens; 54.5 calls per eval task) versus the clone arm's 10{,}800
logical calls (10{,}804 attempts; 14.51\,M tokens; exactly 27 per task)
--- a $2.05\times$ call and $3.73\times$ token ratio driven by the
multi-call panel members; the conservative ceiling at the frozen
break-even price is 1{,}803.82 vs 483.15 CNY (pricing not independently
verifiable; reported as token counts times the frozen 33.3 CNY per 1M
tokens, never as billed amounts). Totals across all arms:
\WpLogicalCalls{} logical calls, \WpHttpAttempts{} attempts,
\WpTotalTokens{} known tokens, \WpCostCny{} CNY ceiling. We therefore
make no realized-cost-matched claim; the common-budget table is the
cost-controlled comparison.

\paragraph{Selector spec (E3).} $\pi_Z$ is multinomial logistic regression
over TF-IDF question features (max 300) and per-member dev accuracies
computed within training folds only; 5-fold task-level CV on the 100 dev
tasks with same-task repeats never split across folds; fold-model
averaging; ties broken by member id; abstain to \bare{} when the top-2
probability gap is below 0.15. Repeat aggregation uses the
available-repeat mean per member--task (same-task repeats stay in one
fold). Comparators: \bare{}, dev-fixed, pre-fixed random member (seeded),
M1b (dev-fixed with bare fallback on missing cells). $E_1$ (budget by
construction) is not applicable because panel members make more than one
call; $E_b$ with $b{=}3$ calls per task is reported as a separately named
estimand. Leakage controls: no answer, correctness, eval-derived
difficulty, post-execution trace, or judge feature is used.
