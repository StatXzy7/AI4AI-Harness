\section{Frozen Selector and Execution-Matched Replay}\label{app:selection}
The selector is fitted on $100$ development tasks. Its recorded choices
and development ranking are evaluated on the common $\RcvPairedN$ tasks.

\subsection{Frozen feature selector}
The reported selector $\pi_Z$ combines five multinomial-logistic
fold models fitted using the $100$ development tasks. Its inputs are
TF-IDF question features (at most $300$) and per-member development
accuracies computed within the respective training folds. All repeats
of a task stay together in the five-fold task partition. The score
summary for member $h$ on development task $x$ is the available-repeat
mean,
\[
 \bar Y_h(x)=\frac{1}{|\mathcal R_{hx}|}
             \sum_{r\in\mathcal R_{hx}}Y(h,x,r),
\]
where $\mathcal R_{hx}$ contains its observed development repeats.
The five fitted models are combined by fold-model averaging. If
$p_k(h\mid x)$ is the probability assigned by fold model $k$, the
inference rule uses $\bar p(h\mid x)=\frac15\sum_{k=1}^5p_k(h\mid x)$.
Let $h_{(1)}$ and $h_{(2)}$ be the members with the two highest averaged
probabilities, breaking ties by member ID. The final frozen choice is
\[
 \pi_Z(x)=
 \begin{cases}
  \text{\bare{}}, & \bar p(h_{(1)}\mid x)-\bar p(h_{(2)}\mid x)<0.15,\\
  h_{(1)}, & \text{otherwise}.
 \end{cases}
\]
Thus the five-fold step supplies the averaged prediction used at
inference, and the threshold is applied after averaging. The policy
table evaluates these final choices, including the baseline fallback.
It reports repeat-mean accuracy, with harness executions as the unit of
evaluation; a selected harness can make multiple model calls.

Comparators are \bare{}, the development-selected fixed member,
one fixed member drawn once with a fixed seed, and the
development-selected member with baseline fallback on missing cells.

\subsection{Selection gain and the scope of budget utility}
For the implemented selector, $Z(x)$ consists only of question features
and training-fold member accuracies. Its gain over the
development-selected fixed member is
\[
 S(\pi_Z)=\mathbb E_x\!\left[
 \bar Y_{\pi_Z(x)}(x)-\bar Y_{h_{\rm dev\text{-}fixed}}(x)\right],
\]
where $\bar Y_h(x)$ now averages the three evaluation repeats. We use
$S$ for policy gain and reserve $D$ for the real-minus-clone repeat
diagnostic. Mechanism labels are not inputs to this feature selector.

Replay budget $b$ counts \emph{harness executions}, each potentially
containing multiple model calls; coverage uses a post-execution oracle.

\subsection{Execution-matched replay and collection accounting}
Each arm contains nine members/slots with three repeats. Replay uses the
common $\RcvPairedN$ tasks and frozen development ranking
(\S\ref{sec:selection-result}), taking all three repeats of the first
$b/3$ members for $b\in\{3,9,15,27\}$. With $f$ incorrect clone
executions among $27$, expected coverage of a uniform size-$b$ subset is
$1-\binom{f}{b}/\binom{27}{b}$, where $\binom{f}{b}=0$ for $b>f$.
Table~\ref{tab:execution-means} compares this exact expectation with
generated-panel coverage at matched execution counts.

Acquisition ledgers retain all attempts and known usage, including
excluded tasks and recovery; outcome tables use the common complete set.
Calls and tokens follow each program and acquisition record.
