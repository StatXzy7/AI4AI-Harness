\begin{abstract}
Large language models are increasingly wrapped in \emph{harnesses}---generated programs of prompts, tools, and control flow---and a growing literature routes over \emph{populations} of such harnesses per query. Routing presupposes that population members disagree behaviorally. We show this presupposition fails for automatically generated populations: on BIRD text-to-SQL with a frozen mid-tier target, the candidates of a standard harness-evolution loop are syntactically diverse (mean pairwise code similarity 0.14) yet behaviorally collapsed (96\% outcome agreement; 21 of 66 candidate pairs identical on every task; the shipped ReAct variant reproduces \bare{}'s final SQL byte-for-byte on every audited task). Execution-trace auditing resolves the collapse into three failure classes: mechanisms described but absent from the control flow, feedback with no causal path to the output, and broken multi-turn paths. A hand-written positive control on the same target, tasks, and judge delivers 7.3\,pp oracle headroom on 151 held-out questions---the headroom routers need is real, and it is the \emph{generation} side that fails. We then probe both axes with a strategy-forced free-form protocol: two of three builders produce populations that exceed pre-specified diversity thresholds at the point estimate for the first time (6.0 and 8.0\,pp headroom; six pairwise-distinct repair sets each), the old un-gated protocol misses, and under fully logged generation the builder axis orders per-strategy acceptance (GLM 3/8, Qwen 7/8, DeepSeek 8/8). The design cannot separate protocol and builder effects. Diversity, however, is necessary, not sufficient: a leave-one-harness-out value predictor over the admitted populations does not beat the best fixed harness ($\leq$0.001 AUROC over task-only features), and in stratified task-held-out controls its repair signal is at chance; its one deployment-relevant success---+6.9\,pp over bare with zero induced harm---holds only as an abstaining gate over the full 24-harness pool. We release a three-metric precondition checklist---pairwise disagreement, union repair, oracle headroom---that harness-generation and harness-routing papers can and should report before routing is attempted.
\end{abstract}
