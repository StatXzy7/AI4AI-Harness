\section*{Appendix Guide}
The appendices document the studies supporting the current claims:
study objects, repeat and selection methods, calibration, and BIRD evidence.
Earlier generation pilots, superseded statistical procedures, gate-development
iterations, and historical BIRD subset-selection analyses are outside this
manuscript's scope and remain in the project archive. Current analysis
amendments and limitations are retained with the affected study.
