\textbf{Protocol sensitivity is the finding.} The Phase-I collapse did not
reproduce under the clean equal-budget protocol: free ungated generation
already produced 8.6\,pp headroom and 31\% union repair. Collapse is a
failure mode of \emph{specific} pipelines, invisible to every code-level
check they passed. Mechanism-verification added no routing value, its
apparent cost largely a population-size artifact: outcome-level auditing,
not stronger code-level gating, detects collapse. Sub-pp effects sit
below the repeat-run noise floor (14.5\% bare any-flip), which belongs
beside every CI. The decompositions show the gate changed \emph{which}
candidates survive, not \emph{whether} the population can answer:
reliability and complementarity need separate reporting.

\textbf{Limitations.} One domain family (text-to-SQL); Phase-I from a
discovery design with disclosed task overlap, a proxy judge, and a
post-trace human control; inference conditional on the six sampled
builders; early Phase-I generations lack per-attempt logs; the analyses
of \S\ref{sec:rich} are exploratory (post-hoc on confirmatory outcomes).
