\textbf{Protocol sensitivity is the finding.} The Phase-I collapse did not
reproduce under the clean equal-budget protocol: free ungated generation
already produced 8.6\,pp headroom and 31\% union repair.
Mechanism-verification added no routing value, its
apparent cost largely a population-size artifact: outcome-level auditing,
not stronger code-level gating, detects collapse. Sub-pp effects sit
below the repeat-run noise floor (14.5\% bare any-flip). The
gate changed \emph{which}
candidates survive, not \emph{whether} the population can answer:
reliability and complementarity need separate reporting.

\textbf{Limitations.} One domain family (text-to-SQL); Phase-I from a
discovery design (disclosed task overlap, proxy judge, post-trace human
control); inference conditional on the six sampled
builders; early Phase-I generations lack per-attempt logs. A
one-seed MATH-500 free-arm replication (Appendix~\ref{app:crossdomain})
reproduces the clean-protocol diversity result in a second domain
(76\% union repair; no generated harness beats bare), but is
exploratory and does not test the collapse-inducing protocol.
