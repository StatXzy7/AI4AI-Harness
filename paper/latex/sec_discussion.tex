\textbf{Central insight and evidence.} Population utility is a stable
task--harness interaction after noise is separated: an oracle asks who
was correct; a router picks beforehand. The diagnostic separates what
the numbers license: ranking is not complementarity, abstention is not
refutation, a plug-in is not calibrated evidence. On MATH-500 the
clone-calibrated gain is $\WpVthreeD$\,pp (CI $\WpVthreeDCI$): no gain
beyond same-code re-execution at the $1$\,pp margin, with coverage-gate
abstention; simulations (\S\ref{sec:calibration}) show what $R{=}3$
resolves.

\textbf{Limitations.} One SQL target, six builders, three seeds
(W1/W3 descriptive); low-call panel filter and previously analyzed
tasks --- no unseen-task claim. Matched-budget selection gains are not
found, by an instrument that does not manufacture complementarity.
