\textbf{The central insight.} Population utility is a stable
task--harness interaction after execution noise is separated, not a
diversity statistic: an oracle asks who happened to be correct; a router
must pick beforehand.

\textbf{What the evidence establishes.} Source, mechanism, and one-run
outcome diversity are distinct measurements. The Phase-I pattern did not
recur in Phase II (protocol changes prevent attribution); the corrected
gate reanalysis shows no clear headroom improvement and does not identify
verification alone; the diagnostic separates what the numbers license:
a stable ranking difference is not complementarity, abstention is not
refutation.

\textbf{What remains unidentified.} Same-code reruns create oracle
gains; flip rates measure instability, not a contrast's standard error.
Clone-controlled repeat validation and cross-mechanism calibration are
needed before attributing headroom to stable capability; the fresh
MATH-500 test (\S\ref{sec:wp1r}) is the first, and it does not pass.

\textbf{Limitations and conclusion.} One text-to-SQL target, six
builders, three seeds; W1/W3 stay descriptive. Practical value requires
selection gains beyond equal-budget same-code sampling --- not found here.
