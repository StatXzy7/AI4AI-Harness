\subsection{Execution variability and the stable-complementarity question}
Independent executions destabilize one-run comparisons: canonical R2
replay finds \RevisionRtwoFlips/28{,}000 same-harness verdict flips
(\RevisionRtwoFlipPercent\%), and the R3 audit found 14.5\% of tasks
changing \bare{} correctness at least once (Appendix~\ref{app:r3});
three bare executions treated as three ``members'' give a descriptive
6.50\,pp one-run oracle gap --- one-run headroom can arise from
execution sampling, not new capability. The stable headroom vanishes
for a same-code clone pool and under global dominance
(Eq.~\ref{eq:identity}); its finite-repeat plug-in is not calibrated
(\S\ref{sec:diagnostics}), so the fresh clone-controlled collection
using the size-controlled procedure follows (\S\ref{sec:wp1r}).
