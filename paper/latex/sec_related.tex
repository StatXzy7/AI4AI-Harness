\subsection{Harness generation for LLMs}
\label{related:generation}
A growing line of work generates task-specific scaffolding around a
frozen model. TTHE evolves text-to-SQL harnesses with an LLM
proposer/judge loop \citep{tthe2026}; Self-Harness \citep{selfharness2026}
shows a model improving its own harness; JIT-Agent \citep{jitagent2026}
learns to generate a harness per task at inference time; and
Task-CoEvolve \citep{coevolve2026} co-evolves tasks and scaffolds.
Automated agent-program search \citep{adas2024} first framed such
generated programs as a search space. A concurrent 2026 cluster makes
harness evolution behavior-aware from the verification side:
HarnessLens \citep{harnesslens2026} allocates rollout budget using
behavior-level checks during evolution, HarnessFix
\citep{harnessfix2026} repairs harnesses from trace-grounded diagnoses,
AHE \citep{ahe2026} verifies edits against self-declared behavioral
predictions, gated quality-diversity/HarnessBank \citep{gatedqd2026}
evolves populations over LLM-assigned pathology descriptors, the Harness
Handbook \citep{handbook2026} targets behavior localization and
outer-loop search, and HarnessForge \citep{harnessforge2026} co-evolves
the harness and the internal policy.

Our unit of analysis and question are complementary but distinct.
Table~\ref{tab:nearest} contrasts the two nearest systems. HarnessLens
uses behavior as a \emph{signal inside} an evolution loop to allocate
compute; we ask whether the resulting \emph{population} is behaviorally
diverse as an outcome property. HarnessBank \citep{gatedqd2026}
documents search-space collapse and screens on LLM-assigned semantic
descriptors; our admission instrument does not score semantics at all,
but drives counterfactual trace probes to verify mechanically that a
declared mechanism executes, and our experiment compares admission
policies through a factorial contrast. Because the implemented gate also
structurally excludes two allowed prompt transformations, the treatment
does not isolate verification's causal contribution.

\subsection{Conditional utility, routing, and repeated sampling}
\label{related:routing}
Harness quality is instance-conditional: \citet{nouniversal2026} and
\citet{nonmonotone2026} document that no single harness dominates across
instances. HELIX evaluates a full sibling-harness outcome matrix and
portfolio complementarity beyond the best fixed candidate but lists a
router as future work \citep{helix2026}. STS routes over six fixed
human-designed scaffolds \citep{sts2026}, GRASP gates skill edits into a
development-time library \citep{grasp2026}, \citet{cfrouting2026} learn
routing over \{no-search, search, abstain\}, and RouteLLM
\citep{routellm2024} routes at the model level. The underlying decision
boundary is the classical distinction between quantitative and
qualitative (crossover) interaction: a change in effect size alone need
not change the preferred action \citep{gailsimon1985}. Algorithm
selection \citep{rice1976} and portfolio solvers such as SATzilla
\citep{xu2008satzilla,kotthoff2016} select enumerated algorithms from
instance features, and randomized restarts have long captured gains from
additional attempts at one solver rather than from a portfolio. Ensemble
selection optimizes a validation objective over a model library
\citep{caruana2004}, a different information set from choosing one
member before execution, and self-consistency
\citep{selfconsistency2022} aggregates multiple sampled trajectories
post hoc, so its cost and information set again differ from
pre-execution single-member routing.

Our candidates are complete automatically generated harnesses, and we
measure the population outcome structure a selector would inherit rather
than build a selector. Post-execution verifiers
\citep{llmverifier2026} score completed trajectories, whereas our
pre-execution estimand chooses a harness before it runs, a different
information set; our oracle coverage is labeled throughout as the
post-execution upper bound a verifier could realize, not as routing
utility.

\subsection{Behavioral diversity in generated populations}
\label{related:diversity}
Diversity of LLM outputs has been studied mainly at the sample level
(pass@$k$ \citep{chen2021codex}, self-consistency, diverse decoding) or
at the agent-policy level. Repeated sampling from a fixed generator and
multi-agent ensembling of independently instantiated identical agents
\citep{moreagents2024} already show performance gains from additional
executions of the same code, and nominally temperature-$0$ calls vary
across repeats \citep{nondeterminism2023}; our same-code clone arm turns
that execution-level baseline into an explicit measured null for harness
populations. Syntactically distinct programs behaving identically is
adjacent to classical program-equivalence \citep{budd1982} and
equivalent-mutant \citep{papadakis2019} results. Most closely,
\citet{metaagents2025} report low behavioral diversity among
auto-designed agents at an aggregate level. We extend that observation in
two directions: a trace-level taxonomy (mechanism described but not
executed, present but untriggered, broken multi-turn) showing exactly
where syntactic difference fails to become an executed strategy, and the
converse finding that identical code under repeated execution produces a
positive apparent-complementarity signal --- measured, for harness
populations, with matched repeats, a same-code control, and explicit
slots/executions/tokens bookkeeping.
