\subsection{Design and primary contrasts}
\label{sec:f2-admission}
The six-builder, three-seed study crosses strategy forcing and gating:
II-A (free, ungated), II-B (free, gated), II-C (forced, ungated), and
II-D (forced, gated). Forced strategies come from a fixed set;
free builders select a declaration within the vocabulary in
Appendix~\ref{app:free-vocabulary}. The evaluation uses $1{,}169$
questions from nine databases, with the disclosed $18$-item smoke-test
exception, and the official BIRD scorer. Results below use the
corrected reanalysis of the pre-outcome specification.
