The boundary results above are statements about what single-run numbers
cannot establish. We package an offline diagnostic on an execution matrix
$Y[h,x,r,c]$ (member, task, repeat, condition) returning one of four
states per class --- SUPPORTED, REFUTED\_WITHIN\_SCOPE,
INSUFFICIENT\_EVIDENCE, NOT\_APPLICABLE --- with scope and minimal
missing evidence, never collapsed into a score: \textbf{(A)} measurement
sufficiency; \textbf{(B)} single-shot coverage; \textbf{(C)} stability
in two estimands; \textbf{(D)} pre-execution selectability (frozen
$\pi_Z$ vs dev-fixed); \textbf{(E)} budget-matched policy utility
($\lambda{=}1$, gated on verified call evidence).

\textbf{Two stability claims.} \emph{C-rank} asks whether some member
pair differs under a simultaneous interval (max-T over all canonical
pairs, family-wise 95\%) with sign-consistent repeats; pair direction is
fixed by member identity, not list position. \emph{C-comp} asks whether
stable task--harness interaction exists. With
$\Delta(x)=p_1(x)-p_0(x)$ and $(u)_+=\max(u,0)$,
\begin{equation}
H_{\rm stable}=\mathbb{E}\,\Delta(x)_+-(\mathbb{E}\Delta(x))_+
=\tfrac12\bigl(\mathbb{E}|\Delta(x)|-|\mathbb{E}\Delta(x)|\bigr),
\label{eq:identity}
\end{equation}
so a member better \emph{everywhere} gives a stable ranking difference
with $H_{\rm stable}{=}0$: global dominance refutes complementarity.
Interaction requires the advantage to \emph{cross} tasks, and any
pre-execution selection gap is bounded by $H_{\rm stable}$. Identity
gates require equal condition fields, constant source hashes, and ---
when matrices coincide --- distinct independent-execution provenance.

\textbf{Why the plug-in cannot decide, and the calibrated test.}
Plugging $R$-repeat averages into $H_{\rm stable}$ maximizes noisy
per-task estimates; resampling tasks cannot remove that selection noise
(Table~\ref{tab:calibration}: the plug-in false-supports at rate
$\approx1$ on every null at $M{=}9,T{=}400,R{=}3$, including $q\equiv0.9$
and the sealed clone arm at $\WpVthreeClonePlugin$\,pp). We cross-fit a
\emph{frozen-selection gain}: fold $v$ freezes on the other repeats both
the per-task mapping $h_v(x)=\arg\max_h\bar q_h(x)$ and the best fixed
member $\bar h_v=\arg\max_h\mathbb E_x\bar q_h(x)$, then scores
$g_v(x)=Y_{h_v(x),x,v}-Y_{\bar h_v,x,v}$ on the held-out repeat,
$G=\mathbb E_{x,v}g_v(x)$.\label{sec:calibration-test}
Under dominance the frozen mapping is the best fixed member ($G\le0$),
and $0\le G\le H_{\rm stable}$. The decision statistic is
$D=G_{\rm real}-G_{\rm clone}$ on identical tasks, with a two-arm
Monte Carlo randomization test ($+1$; labels permuted independently
per task and repeat in both arms) and a paired task bootstrap. The
reference is exact at equal ability; over the rest of the dominance
cone finite-sample false-support is verified empirically in the grid
below, not asserted analytically. Without a clone arm $G$ is
descriptive and no SUPPORTED is emitted; a $\le10\%$ incomplete-task
coverage gate and extremal unknown-cell bounds complete the rule.

\textbf{Known-truth validation.}\label{sec:calibration}
We simulate binary tensors from specified $M\times T$ probability
matrices: iid equal ability (constant and heterogeneous-difficulty),
global dominance (including the $0.5/0.9$ configuration under which the
old vs-\bare{} statistic spuriously rejects), and crossover
alternatives, over $M\in\{3,9,20\}$, $T\in\{100,400,1000\}$,
$R\in\{3,5,10,20\}$, and MCAR/hardness-related missingness.
Table~\ref{tab:calibration}: v3 stays nominal on every null while the
plug-in false-supports almost always; a clean two-specialist crossover
($H{=}17.5$\,pp) has $0/1\%$ power at $R{=}3/5$ but $100\%$ at
$R{=}10,20$, while realistic cyclic interactions of $1$--$4$\,pp stay
unresolved --- an honest abstention at three repeats. Twelve
hand controls and two auditor challenges match their hash-frozen
states, with invariant tests for permutation invariance and
dominance$\ne$complementarity, and the old rule survives only as the
descriptive plug-in. A hash-bound re-execution also corrected a 14-flip
verification claim (Appendix~\ref{app:diagnostics}); on the archives
C/D/E abstain by construction.
