The boundary results above are statements about what single-run numbers
cannot establish. To make that boundary checkable, we package an offline
diagnostic on an execution matrix $Y[h,x,r,c]$ (member, task, repeat,
condition) that returns one of four states per class --- SUPPORTED,
REFUTED\_WITHIN\_SCOPE, INSUFFICIENT\_EVIDENCE, NOT\_APPLICABLE --- with
scope and minimal missing evidence, never collapsed into a score:
\textbf{(A)} measurement sufficiency; \textbf{(B)} single-shot coverage;
\textbf{(C)} stability in two estimands; \textbf{(D)} pre-execution
selectability (frozen deterministic $\pi_Z$ versus dev-fixed);
\textbf{(E)} budget-matched policy utility ($\lambda{=}1$ harm weighting,
gated on verified per-task call evidence).

\textbf{Two different stability claims.} Class C separates what earlier
sections conflated. \emph{C-rank} asks whether some member pair has a
same-condition accuracy difference whose interval excludes zero with
sign-consistent repeats; its pair direction is fixed by member identity, not
list position, so the population conclusion is structurally invariant to
member ordering. \emph{C-comp} asks whether stable task--harness interaction
exists. For two members with per-task correct-probability gap
$\Delta(x)=p_1(x)-p_0(x)$, writing $(u)_+ = \max(u, 0)$,
\begin{equation}
H_{\rm stable} \;=\; \mathbb{E}\,\Delta(x)_+ - \bigl(\mathbb{E}\,\Delta(x)\bigr)_+
\;=\; \tfrac{1}{2}\bigl(\mathbb{E}\,|\Delta(x)| - |\mathbb{E}\,\Delta(x)|\bigr),
\label{eq:identity}
\end{equation}
so a member that is better everywhere yields a perfectly stable ranking
difference with $H_{\rm stable}=0$: global dominance is the \emph{refutation}
of complementarity, not evidence for it. Real interaction requires the
per-task advantage to \emph{cross} tasks; with pre-execution features $Z$
and $q_h(Z)=\mathbb{E}[p_h(X)\mid Z]$, any achievable selection gap
$\mathbb{E}_Z\max_h q_h(Z)-\max_h\mathbb{E}_Z q_h(Z)$ is bounded by
$H_{\rm stable}$. Identity gates refuse to score stability at
all when purported repeats differ in execution-condition fields, in member
source hashes, or contain byte-identical outcome matrices (cloned
``repeats'').

\textbf{Finite-repeat bias control.} A plug-in of repeat-averaged
accuracies is upward-biased whenever finite-$R$ noise can change the
per-task argmax, so stability is never scored from the plug-in alone:
cross-fitted discovery/validation plus calibration against the
equal-budget same-code clone null (same finite-$R$ mechanism), with
identity gates refusing hash-matched repeats (Appendix~\ref{app:diagnostics}).

\textbf{Selectability and budgeted utility, formalized.} Class D scores
a frozen deterministic pre-execution policy
$D(\pi_Z)=\mathbb{E}_x[Y(x,\pi_Z(x))-Y(x,h_{\rm dev\text{-}fixed})]$;
Class E scores $U_b(\pi)=\mathbb{E}_x[\Delta(x,\pi(x))]$ under a uniform
call budget with $\lambda{=}1$ harm weighting, admissible only with
per-record budget evidence. Full definitions and the MATH-500 evaluation
are in Appendix~\ref{app:diagnostics} and \S\ref{sec:wp1r}.

\textbf{Validation by invariants, not by output assertions.} The frozen
package pairs eight mechanism-derived classes (16 controls) with four
repeat-based classes (8 controls) exercising global dominance (C-rank
SUPPORTED, C-comp REFUTED), equal-mean crossover skill (C-rank
INSUFFICIENT, C-comp SUPPORTED), and condition-mixing/cloned-repeat
traps. Each class has two frozen instances: instance one runs in
calibration, instance two (12 controls) plus two auditor challenges in
the blinded phase, whose $14\times 6$ state cells all match expectation:
zero false supports, positive control recognized, abstention cap holds.
Expected states derive from the generating mechanisms and are hash-frozen
before the blinded run; invariant tests assert permutation invariance and
dominance$\neq$complementarity directly. One recorded challenge ``failure'' from an earlier
unbound executor is resolved by spec-faithful re-execution; the stored
file's own numbers are unreachable under the spec it cites and are retained
only as provenance.

\textbf{The diagnostic audits its own history.} Hash-bound re-execution
corrected one of our own earlier verification claims --- an
endpoint-sensitive rescore of 14{,}400 archived MATH verdicts produces 14
flips, not the previously reported zero (Appendix~\ref{app:diagnostics}).
On the archives C/D/E abstain by construction: BIRD has no matched
repeats or dev split, and MATH shows coverage without identifiable
selection.
