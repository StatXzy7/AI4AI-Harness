\subsection{Pre-Execution Selection and Oracle Replay}\label{sec:utility}
\paragraph{The frozen selector matches the development-selected baseline.}
The final feature-policy choices, including its confidence-based
fallback, are \bare{} on all $\RcvPairedN$ evaluation tasks. The result
evaluates this complete decision rule; the reported choices do not
separate the classifier's initial preference from fallback decisions.
Keeping those choices fixed and updating only the
recorded outcomes gives $\RcvBaselineAcc\%$ accuracy over three repeats,
identical to the development-selected fixed member:
$\RcvPolicyGain$ pp gain for the tested policy and features. Appendix~\ref{app:selection}
retains its construction and the same-set comparator results.

\paragraph{At the full execution budget, the oracle coverages tie.}
\label{sec:selection-result}
We compare development-ranked portfolios with uniformly selected
same-code executions on the $\RcvPairedN$ tasks complete in both arms.
At $b=3$, the portfolio contains only \bare{}, so its
$\RcvReplayThreeGap$ pp difference reflects execution variation
between identical programs.
At $b=9$ and $15$, the differences are $\RcvReplayNineGap$ pp and
$\RcvReplayFifteenGap$ pp. At $b=27$, both arms reach
$\RcvReplayFullCoverage\%$ oracle coverage (Table~\ref{tab:execution-means}).
