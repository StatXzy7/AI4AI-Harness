\subsection{Pre-Execution Selection and Oracle Replay}\label{sec:utility}
\paragraph{Pre-execution selection yields no gain.}
A frozen feature-based policy tests a choice that can actually be made
before execution. On the repeat panel, it selects \bare{} on all
$400$ evaluation tasks and ties the development-selected fixed member
at $\WpPiZAcc\%$ repeat-mean accuracy: $\WpPiZDiff$\,\% gain.
The pre-fixed random-member comparator differs by
$\WpRandomDiff$\,\% (Appendix~\ref{app:selection}). The policy avoids weak
members but obtains no task-specific benefit. This result concerns
one policy and feature set; it neither exhausts possible selectors
nor turns the oracle opportunity into achieved accuracy.

\paragraph{Execution-matched oracle replay.}
\label{sec:selection-result}
Even oracle coverage must be compared with the extra attempts a fixed
program could receive. The replay gives a development-ranked panel
portfolio and the clone arm the same number $b$ of harness executions.
At $b=3$, the panel contains only \bare{}; its $+0.37$\,\% difference
cannot be due to program diversity. Adding members leaves a
$+0.03$\,\% difference at $b=9$, followed by negative differences
at $b=15$ and $27$ (Table~\ref{tab:execution-means}).

\centering\small
\begin{tabular}{@{}lrrrr@{}}
\toprule
Harness executions per task $b$ & 3 & 9 & 15 & 27 \\
\midrule
Panel members, including \bare{} & 1 & 3 & 5 & 9 \\
Panel oracle coverage (\%) & 98.00 & 98.25 & 98.25 & 98.25 \\
Clone oracle coverage (\%) & 97.63 & 98.22 & 98.36 & 98.50 \\
Panel minus clones (\%) & $+0.37$ & $+0.03$ & $-0.11$ & $-0.25$ \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabular}
\caption{\textbf{The full execution budget does not favor more programs
in the observed oracle mean.} Members are ranked on development data
and contribute three repeats each. Clone coverage averages subsets
of its $27$ recorded executions. Both arms use post-execution oracle

\label{tab:execution-means}
\end{table}

Collection also costs more for the generated arm: $22{,}192$ logical
calls and $54.17$M known tokens versus $10{,}800$ calls and $14.51$M
for clones, a $3.73\times$ token ratio. These are realized collection
costs, not a token-matched performance comparison. Cross-arm majority
voting is not reported because answer formats are incompatible.
Thus the tested selector delivers no gain, and the execution-matched
oracle means provide no consistent advantage for this portfolio.
Neither result establishes how other selectors or a token-matched
operational comparison would perform.
