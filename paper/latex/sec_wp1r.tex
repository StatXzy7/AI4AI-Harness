\subsection{Fresh clone-controlled evidence on MATH-500}\label{sec:wp1r}

The archives lack matched repeats and a same-code control, so they
cannot separate stable complementarity from one-run sampling luck. A
pre-registered collection (frozen 2026-09-15; Appendix~\ref{app:wp1r})
executes a frozen eight-harness panel plus \bare{} on the 400 MATH-500
tasks for three repeats, and nine \emph{equal-slot} byte-identical
\bare{} clone slots for the same repeats; we reserve ``equal budget''
for the common execution-budget comparison below.

\paragraph{Estimands and results.}
We keep three quantities separate: (i) the descriptive repeat-averaged
plug-in $\widehat H$ ($\WpVthreePlugin$\,pp here, and also positive at
$\WpVthreeClonePlugin$\,pp on the clone arm --- known-truth simulations
show it false-supports under pure noise); (ii) the cross-fitted
frozen-selection gain $G$ against the best \emph{fixed} member, never
against \bare{} ($G_{\rm real}=\WpVthreeG$\,pp, CI $\WpVthreeGCI$;
$G_{\rm clone}=\WpVthreeGclone$\,pp, so finite-$(R,T)$ selection noise
is of the same order as the panel's gain); (iii) the calibrated
real-minus-clone difference $D=\WpVthreeD$\,pp (CI $\WpVthreeDCI$,
randomization $p=\WpVthreeP$) on \WpVthreeNPaired{} paired complete
tasks. C-rank is \textsc{\WpCrankState} with multiplicity-adjusted
intervals. The coverage gate fails ($12.5\%$ real-arm tasks excluded,
above the frozen $10\%$), so C-comp is \textsc{\WpVthreeState}: not a
proof of absence, and the interval upper end does not establish
equivalence at $1$\,pp; unknown-cell extrema bracket $G$ at
$\WpVthreeBounds$\,pp.

\paragraph{Common execution budget.}
Slots do not determine spend. Offline from the sealed repeats,
Table~\ref{tab:budget-curves} compares the dev-ranked panel portfolio
with $b$ of the 27 same-code executions at $b=3,6,\ldots,27$:
post-execution oracle coverage is tied at every budget
($-0.25$ to $+0.37$\,pp). We report no cross-arm majority-vote
accuracy: the arms emit incompatible answer formats (boxed numbers vs
free-form prose), so no modal answer key is fair across styles;
arm-internal replays stay in the artifact. The panel used
$3.7\times$ the clone arm's tokens, so no realized-cost-matched claim
is made (Appendix~\ref{app:wp1r}).

\paragraph{Selector utility (E3).} The frozen policy $\pi_Z$ abstains
to \bare{} on every eval task and ties dev-fixed at \WpBareAcc\%
(paired $\WpPiZDiff$\,pp, CI $\WpPiZDiffCI$); a pre-fixed random member
is materially worse ($\WpRandomDiff$\,pp, CI $\WpRandomCI$;
Table~\ref{tab:wp1r-policy}). Class D gives no usable selectability and
the $b{=}3$ utility $U_b$ is \WpEbZero{}-gain for every frozen policy.
