AI-generated harness populations can be exactly what their source files
suggest and nothing like what routing needs. We measured that gap on a
genuinely distinct-source population --- code similarity shows no observed rank
association with behavioral similarity --- explained its mechanism at the trace
level, and showed the missing headroom exists on the task. We separated genuine
collapse from generation-null failures, and froze a confirmatory protocol: a
$2{\times}2$ factorial (strategy forcing $\times$ counterfactual conformance
gate) over six builders and three seeds, evaluated on 1{,}169 questions from
databases never opened during development, under the official BIRD scorer.

The confirmatory answer is two-fold. The primary contrast is null: the combined
strategy-forced, conformance-gated protocol does not increase population headroom
over free, ungated generation ($-0.16$\,pp, 95\% CI $[-0.90, +0.82]$;
permutation $p=0.65$). The factorial decomposition explains why, and delivers the
study's sharpest finding: \textbf{the conformance gate alone reduces headroom}
($-0.67$\,pp, CI $[-1.17, -0.13]$), strategy forcing alone is null ($+0.41$\,pp,
CI $[-0.22, +1.11]$), and their interaction is negative ($-0.87$\,pp) --- the two
components substitute rather than complement. Mechanism fidelity and population
complementarity trade against each other: gate-rejected candidates --- valid code
whose declared mechanism did not survive counterfactual probing --- were net
contributors to headroom. Both arms carry substantial headroom (3.1--11.9\,pp),
far from the Phase-I collapse regime: under a clean equal-budget protocol, free
generation already produces headroom-rich populations at these builder strengths.

Phase-I controls additionally showed open-set harness-value prediction does not beat
the best fixed harness, so diversity is necessary but not sufficient. The practical
output stands: measure disagreement, union repair, and harm before routing over a
generated population --- and do not assume that verifying what a harness claims to
do will make the population more useful.
