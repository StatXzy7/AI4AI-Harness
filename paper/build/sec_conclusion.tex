AI-generated harness populations can be exactly what their source files suggest
and nothing like what routing needs --- and the reverse can also hold. Phase I
measured the syntactic--behavioral gap in a discovery population, traced its
mechanisms at the trace level, and showed the missing headroom exists on the
task. A pre-specified confirmatory factorial study (six builders, three seeds,
$2{\times}2$ forcing $\times$ gate, 1{,}169 unseen questions, official
scorer) then revised the story twice. \textbf{Collapse proved protocol-sensitive}:
under the clean equal-budget protocol, free ungated generation already produced
headroom-rich populations --- collapse is a failure mode of specific pipelines,
invisible to every code-level check they passed. \textbf{The intervention was
null within measurement precision}: no clear improvement on the primary
contrast; the apparent negative gate effect substantially size-confounded and
not Holm-significant; the 14.5\% bare-outcome noise floor exceeds the
sub-pp effects in question.

Limitations: one domain family; Phase-I from a discovery design;
inference conditional on six sampled builders; R2 pending.
The practical output stands --- measure disagreement, repair, harm, and
headroom before routing over a generated population, and audit the
generation protocol itself.
