AI-generated harness populations can be exactly what their source files suggest
and nothing like what routing needs --- and the reverse can also hold. Phase I
measured the syntactic--behavioral gap in a discovery population, traced its
mechanisms, and showed the missing headroom exists on the task. A
pre-specified confirmatory factorial study (six builders, three seeds,
$2{\times}2$ forcing $\times$ gate, 1{,}169 unseen questions) then revised the
story twice. \textbf{Collapse proved protocol-sensitive}: a failure mode of
specific pipelines, invisible to every code-level check they passed.
\textbf{The intervention was null within measurement precision}: no
primary-contrast improvement; the apparent negative gate effect
size-confounded and not Holm-significant; the 14.5\% bare and 8.5\%
cache-off noise floors exceed the sub-pp effects in question. The
practical output stands: measure disagreement, repair, harm, and headroom
before routing, and audit the generation protocol itself.
