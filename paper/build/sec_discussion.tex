\textbf{Protocol sensitivity is the finding.} The Phase-I collapse did not
reproduce under the clean equal-budget protocol: free ungated generation
already produced populations with 8.6\,pp oracle headroom and 31\% union
repair. Collapse is a failure mode of \emph{specific} generation pipelines,
invisible to every code-level check they passed. Adding
mechanism-verification did not add routing value, and its apparent cost was
largely a population-size artifact: outcome-level auditing, not stronger
code-level gating, is what detects collapse. Sub-pp generation-side effects
sit below the repeat-run noise floor (14.5\% bare any-flip), which belongs
beside every CI. The yield and headroom decompositions show the gate
changed \emph{which} candidates survive, not \emph{whether} the
population can answer: reliability and complementarity need separate
reporting.\textbf{Limitations.} One domain family (text-to-SQL); Phase-I from a
discovery design with disclosed task overlap, a proxy judge, and a
post-trace human control; inference conditional on the six sampled
builders; early Phase-I generations lack per-attempt logs.
