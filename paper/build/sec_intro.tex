The fastest-growing lever on a frozen LLM is no longer weights but the
\emph{harness} --- the program of prompts, tools, and control flow that wraps
the model for a task. A growing line of ``AI4AI'' systems generates this
scaffolding automatically: LLM builders propose, edit, and evolve harness code.
The implicit premise is that when the generated code differs, the
\emph{behavior} differs --- that a population of thirty harnesses is thirty
ways of answering, not one way written thirty times. Routing and value
prediction over such populations presuppose exactly this.

\textbf{Phase I (discovery): the premise fails, and for identifiable
reasons.} On BIRD text-to-SQL \citep{bird2021} with a frozen mid-tier target,
we measure what the candidates of a standard harness-evolution loop actually
\emph{do}, not what their source files say (Figure~\ref{fig:phenomenon}).
Every candidate passes a code-difference check, yet among the 12 generated
candidates (66 candidate pairs) average pairwise outcome disagreement is
\textbf{3.8\%} and \textbf{21 pairs are behaviorally identical}; counting the
shipped \react{} harness and \bare{} baseline as well (91 pairs), 36 pairs
return identical correctness on every task, and pairwise code similarity is
uncorrelated with outcome disagreement (Spearman $\rho=-0.01$). The shipped
\react{} variant reproduces \bare's final SQL byte-for-byte on every audited
task. Execution-trace auditing resolves ``different code'' into three failure
classes: mechanisms \emph{described} but absent from the control flow,
feedback with no causal path to the output, and genuinely multi-turn paths
that are broken (\S\ref{sec:phase1}). We call this \textbf{behavioral
collapse}.

Collapse is not a property of the task: four hand-written harnesses with
forcibly distinct execution paths deliver 7.3\,pp oracle headroom on the same
target and judge --- the headroom routers need is real; the generation side
fails to populate it. We also document a second, more primitive failure mode
in one deployed pipeline: a widely used optimize loop emitted candidates
\emph{byte-identical to the baseline source}. This is not behavioral collapse
--- no distinct population was generated at all --- and we report it as a
\emph{generation-null} (M0) failure, kept strictly separate from the
collapse claim, which we support only on genuinely distinct-source
populations.

\textbf{Phase II (confirmatory): a pre-registered factorial.} Phase I
suggested that behavioral collapse can be repaired by generation-side
interventions, but its design --- two nested contrasts, three builders,
unlogged early runs --- could not separate protocol from builder effects, its
evaluation set overlapped development items, and its judge was a conservative
proxy. We therefore froze a confirmatory protocol before any confirmatory
outcome was observed: a $2{\times}2$ factorial over \emph{strategy forcing}
(assigned mechanism vs.\ free invention) and a \emph{mechanism-conformance
gate} (counterfactual trace verification vs.\ neutral validity only), crossed
with six builder models and three seeds, evaluated on 1{,}169 questions from
nine databases never opened during development, scored by the official BIRD
judge, with the primary contrast (forced+gated vs.\ free+ungated populations)
analyzed as paired continuous effects with hierarchical bootstrap intervals
(\S\ref{sec:phase2}). The conformance instruments are calibrated against
hand-written positive controls and known-negative impostor harnesses before
any candidate is scored.

Our contributions:
\begin{enumerate}
\def\labelenumi{\arabic{enumi}.}
\tightlist
\item
  \textbf{An outcome-matrix measurement of behavioral collapse} in
  AI-generated harness populations, with an execution-trace taxonomy
  explaining why code diversity fails to become behavioral diversity, and a
  clean separation between genuine collapse and generation-null failures.
\item
  \textbf{Calibrated generation-side instruments and a pre-registered
  factorial study}: a counterfactual conformance suite and self-specified
  behavioral contracts, both calibrated on positive and negative controls;
  a $2{\times}2$ design with equal raw generation budgets that isolates the
  combined intervention and its components on uncontaminated data under the
  official scorer.
\item
  \textbf{A boundary result for routing}: on the Phase-I populations,
  open-set harness-value prediction does not beat the best fixed harness,
  and its apparent per-task signal is consistent with task memorization;
  we scope this precisely to the tested predictors and splits
  (\S\ref{sec:boundary}).
\end{enumerate}

Beyond harnesses, the failure pattern we isolate --- LLM builders producing
plausible, well-documented, behaviorally inert variants --- is a
generation-reliability phenomenon we expect to recur wherever a model is
asked to modify its own program.
