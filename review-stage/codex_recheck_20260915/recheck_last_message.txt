## Remaining blocking issues

### 1. E can support without verified call records

At [`core.py`](E:/projects/AI4AI-Harness/experiment/diagnostics/core.py:569), missing `calls` produces a `cost_evidence` label, but does not force E to abstain. The function can continue to return `SUPPORTED` at line 599.

Reproducible example: synthetic C4 and CH1 have no per-record calls yet E is `SUPPORTED`; CH1’s historical detail explicitly says `cost_evidence: "calls_missing"`.

**Impact:** This violates the frozen E contract, which requires insufficient evidence when call counts cannot be verified. It weakens every positive E result in synthetic validation.

**Minimal fix:** Require complete per-record call evidence before allowing E `SUPPORTED`; alternatively, explicitly amend the plan to define a design-by-construction exception, then regenerate controls and manuscript wording.

### 2. D implementation does not implement the frozen five-fold-CV policy

The frozen plan specifies five-fold cross-validation for the selection policy. [`core.py`](E:/projects/AI4AI-Harness/experiment/diagnostics/core.py:490)–[`core.py`](E:/projects/AI4AI-Harness/experiment/diagnostics/core.py:524) fits once on the entire development split. No fold-based training occurs; `dev_acc` is computed but unused.

**Impact:** D/E artifacts validate a different estimator from the frozen protocol. The control pass therefore cannot establish conformance to the stated D estimand.

**Minimal fix:** Implement the specified CV procedure and rerun all controls/challenges, or record a formal amendment in v2 and relabel all affected evidence.

### 3. Condition identity comparison is not type-safe

[`core.py`](E:/projects/AI4AI-Harness/experiment/diagnostics/core.py:318)–[`core.py`](E:/projects/AI4AI-Harness/experiment/diagnostics/core.py:323) compares `str(value)`. Thus `{"timeout": 60}` and `{"timeout": "60"}` are treated as identical conditions.

**Impact:** A claimed P0-2 refusal guarantee is not universal.

**Minimal fix:** Compare canonical typed JSON values, preserving type and nested structure, and add an integer/string mismatch test.

### 4. C-comp mishandles missing repeat outcomes

[`core.py`](E:/projects/AI4AI-Harness/experiment/diagnostics/core.py:399) replaces missing repeat averages with zero before calculating `H_stable`. This can create artificial complementarity or artificial dominance when cells are unknown.

**Minimal fix:** Propagate missingness into C-comp bounds or abstain whenever the relevant per-task member expectation is unidentified.
