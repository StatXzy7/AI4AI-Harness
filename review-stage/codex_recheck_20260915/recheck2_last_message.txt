## Blocking issues

1. **D’s “five-fold CV” is implemented incorrectly.**  
   In [`core.py:529-534`](E:/projects/AI4AI-Harness/experiment/diagnostics/core.py:529), each model is trained with `fold_of == f`, i.e. on the held-out fold. The frozen amendment requires training on the other four folds (`fold_of != f`) and averaging eval predictions. The implementation therefore validates a different estimator.

   The implementation also omits the frozen dev-accuracy feature vector: [`core.py:541-547`](E:/projects/AI4AI-Harness/experiment/diagnostics/core.py:541) constructs only stratum one-hot features, while the frozen plan requires dev-member accuracies plus metadata ([plan:27](E:/projects/AI4AI-Harness/review-stage/DIAGNOSTIC_PLAN_20260915.md:27)).

   The added CV test is insufficient: [`test_diagnostics.py:331-345`](E:/projects/AI4AI-Harness/experiment/diagnostics/test_diagnostics.py:331) checks shape and a module name, but never compares predictions with a correct five-fold implementation.

2. **The E budget gate still accepts unverifiable budgets.**  
   [`core.py:627-628`](E:/projects/AI4AI-Harness/experiment/diagnostics/core.py:627) treats any nonempty `calls` dictionary as verified, including `aggregate_only`, partial records, or records exceeding the one-call budget. It also accepts `calls_status="per_record"` with an empty or incomplete dictionary.

   The frozen contract requires complete per-task evidence and a verified ≤1-call budget ([plan:28](E:/projects/AI4AI-Harness/review-stage/DIAGNOSTIC_PLAN_20260915.md:28)). The tests cover only empty archive evidence, construction-by-design, and an all-ones synthetic mapping; they do not cover aggregate-only, partial, or over-budget records.

3. **Condition identity has a residual missing-vs-null loophole.**  
   The `60` versus `"60"` case is correctly fixed at [`core.py:327-330`](E:/projects/AI4AI-Harness/experiment/diagnostics/core.py:327), but `condition={}` and `condition={"timeout": null}` both become `.get("timeout) -> None`. Canonicalizing the whole typed condition object, rather than per-field `.get()`, is needed for complete identity semantics.
