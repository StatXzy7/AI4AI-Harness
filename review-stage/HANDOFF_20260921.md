## Integrity rules (the external reviewer should verify these)

1. The exploratory **4.75pp** one-run headroom (35 harnesses, one run) and the
   controlled **-0.10pp** clone-calibrated D (8-harness panel, R=3) are
   separate populations/estimands; the paper never frames one as correcting
   or eliminating the other.
2. The descriptive plugins **2.54pp / 2.67pp** are on different complete-task
   sets (350 real / 383 clone) and are not a paired contrast; the paired
   statistic is only D on 342 tasks. G_clone is **0.55**, not 2.67.
3. Calibration is stated as an **empirical, tested-DGP** result (100 cells, 0
   errors, worst null false-support 2%, Wilson [0.68,5.71]%), with the Monte
   Carlo randomization reference exact only at the equal-ability exchangeable
   null — never an analytic finite-sample size claim over the dominance cone.
4. `H_stable = 1/2(E|Δ|−|EΔ|)` is presented as a two-member **population**
   identity and conceptual anchor, not a finite-sample bound on G.
5. C-comp is **Insufficient Evidence** for two disclosed reasons — the
   coverage gate fails (12.5% > 10%) and power at R=3 is 0% even for an
   H=17.5pp crossover — not a negative and not 1pp equivalence (CI upper
   end 1.46).
6. Slots, executions, and tokens are reported separately; no realized
   cost-matched claim (panel 3.73× tokens); no cross-arm majority-vote claim
   (boxed-number vs free-form answer formats).
7. No numbers were hand-edited: every value comes from the generated macros
   (`revision_numbers.tex`, `wp1r_numbers.tex`) and was cross-checked against
   `artifacts/diagnostics/calibration_v3/calibration_results.json`,
   `artifacts/wp1r_20260915/analysis_report.json`, and
   `artifacts/phase2/fig1_phenomenon_data.json`.
